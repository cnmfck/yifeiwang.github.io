Writing on film电影与媒体写作essays

Eight short essays for four film and media courses, built on about fifty sources and read alongside psychology.

四门电影与媒体课，八篇短论文，引用约五十种文献，并且常常和心理学一起读。

FILM 335, Culture and Technology文化与技术

Culture jamming has a shelf life文化干扰有保质期

Culture jamming works by making familiar media look strange. The essay argues that this power wears off: once a jam format becomes familiar and profitable, marketing absorbs it.文化干扰靠的是把熟悉的媒体变得陌生。文章认为这种力量会过期：一种干扰手法一旦变得眼熟、能赚钱，就会被营销吸收。

The argument论点

Appropriation shows that “transparent” images feel true because they are familiar, not because they are true. Apocalypse Pooh and the Yes Men break that familiarity and make viewers ask questions.挪用说明，所谓“透明”的影像让人觉得真实，是因为熟悉，而不是因为真实。《Apocalypse Pooh》和 Yes Men 打破了这种熟悉感，让观众开始追问。

The psychology心理学

From PSYC 343: easy-to-process information feels true (processing fluency), and difficulty can switch on slow, analytic thinking. Repetition makes things fluent again, which is why every jam eventually stops feeling strange.来自 PSYC 343（判断与决策）：容易处理的信息让人觉得真（加工流畅性），而处理困难会启动慢速的分析式思考。重复会让信息重新变得流畅，所以任何干扰最终都会不再显得陌生。

The test检验

Show viewers the same jam five times: critical comments should fall each time, but more slowly for viewers personally tied to its target. In Japan, where the mushroom cloud means real victims, #NoBarbenheimer pushed Warner Bros. Japan to apologize.让观众把同一个干扰作品看五遍：批评性评论应该一次比一次少，但和作品对象有切身联系的观众下降得更慢。在日本，蘑菇云意味着真实的受害者，#NoBarbenheimer 让华纳日本道歉了。

Transparent messages are not transparent, only familiar.透明的信息并不透明，只是熟悉。

Cases案例Apocalypse Pooh (1987), RiP!: A Remix Manifesto (2008), Barbie and Oppenheimer (2023)

Read with参考Reber and Schwarz, Alter et al., MacKenzie, Harold, ManovichReber 与 Schwarz、Alter 等、MacKenzie、Harold、Manovich

FILM 215

Science Fiction Cinema科幻电影

Gender and domestic crisis in 1950s science fiction1950 年代科幻片里的性别与家庭危机

Cold War science fiction let women be competent, like the scientist in Them!, then framed that competence as a threat. The films manage social change instead of celebrating it, and put the family back in charge once the monster is gone.冷战时期的科幻片让女性有能力，比如《Them!》里的女科学家，但又把这种能力写成威胁。这些电影是在“管理”社会变化而不是欢迎它，怪物一除，家庭秩序就被重新确立。

Films影片Them! (1954), Invasion of the Body Snatchers (1956)

Read with参考Telotte, Susan A. George, BookerTelotte、Susan A. George、Booker

Hypermasculinity and instability in 1980s cyborg films1980 年代赛博格电影里的超男性气质与不稳定

RoboCop and the Terminator look like pure male power, but a body that needs machinery to stay strong is also a body that has lost control. The exaggerated strength is a response to a masculinity already in crisis.机械战警和终结者看起来是纯粹的男性力量，但要靠机器才能强大的身体，本身就失去了掌控。夸张的力量，恰恰是对已经陷入危机的男性气质的补偿。

Films影片RoboCop (1987), The Terminator (1984)

Read with参考Telotte, Fernbach, Booker, NamaTelotte、Fernbach、Booker、Nama

FILM 260

Digital Media Theory and Practice数字媒体理论与实践

Old film, new logic: Run Lola Run as database cinema旧电影，新逻辑：作为“数据库电影”的《罗拉快跑》

A feature film with a fixed running time can still think like a database. Lola’s three runs are alternatives to compare, so viewers ask “what changed?” instead of “what happens next?” If platforms ever serve different cuts to different viewers, the platform decides which alternatives exist.一部片长固定的故事片，也可以按数据库的逻辑组织。罗拉跑了三次，观众比较的是三个版本，问的是“哪里变了”，而不是“接下来会怎样”。如果平台将来给不同观众推送不同剪辑，决定哪些版本存在的就是平台。

Films影片Run Lola Run (1998)

Read with参考Manovich, Bordwell, Kittler, McLuhanManovich、Bordwell、Kittler、McLuhan

Selective visibility: how new media shape surveillance and safety选择性的可见：新媒体如何塑造监控与安全感

New media mix top-down watching with peers watching each other, and they make some dangers easier to see than others. Phone alerts make some risks feel common, which is the availability heuristic at work. Their absence does not mean a place is safer.新媒体把自上而下的监视和同伴之间的互相观看叠在一起，也让一些危险比另一些更“看得见”。手机警报让某些风险显得更常见，这正是可得性启发在起作用；没有警报，也不等于更安全。

Read with参考Foucault, Andrejevic, Tversky and Kahneman, Browne, ZuboffFoucault、Andrejevic、Tversky 与 Kahneman、Browne、Zuboff

FILM 303

World Cinemas世界电影

From factory floor to football pitch: realism and the remaking of Britishness从工厂车间到足球场：现实主义与“英国性”的重塑

British realism moved its conflict from the factory (Kitchen Sink) to the benefits office (Loach) and the family (Leigh), then Bend It Like Beckham answered it with colour and joy. The argument: realism grows less truthful when its look stays fixed after the nation has changed.英国现实主义的冲突从工厂（厨房水槽电影）转到福利办公室（Loach），再转到家庭（Leigh）；《我爱贝克汉姆》则用色彩和喜悦回应了它。论点是：国家已经变了，现实主义的样子却不变，它就会变得不那么真实。

Films影片Saturday Night and Sunday Morning (1960), I, Daniel Blake (2016), Secrets & Lies (1996), Bend It Like Beckham (2002)

Read with参考John Hill, Samantha Lay, Forrest and Merrington, O’Sullivan, ChackoJohn Hill、Samantha Lay、Forrest 与 Merrington、O’Sullivan、Chacko

Unequal speeds: from porous solidarity to human commodification不平等的速度：从松动的团结到人的商品化

Time-space compression (Harvey) is the engine of liquid modernity (Bauman), but it does not make everyone equally mobile. Together shows collective bonds starting to leak; Lilja 4-ever shows the end point, a girl moved across a border as cargo. Whose mobility still counts as freedom?时空压缩（Harvey）是流动的现代性（Bauman）的发动机，但它并没有让每个人同样自由地流动。《Together》里集体的纽带开始松动；《Lilja 4-ever》是终点：一个女孩像货物一样被运过边境。谁的流动还算自由？

Films影片Together (2000), Lilja 4-ever (2002)

Read with参考Harvey, Bauman, Doreen Massey, LarssonHarvey、Bauman、Doreen Massey、Larsson

FILM 335

Culture and Technology文化与技术

Technology and the conditions of cultural democratization技术与文化民主化的条件

Technology democratizes culture only when it shares out the chance to contribute and to be heard. Owning a camera is not the same as having the skills, and permission to upload is not the same as being found. The essay ends with a prediction that could be tested.技术只有在真正分配出“参与”和“被听见”的机会时，才算让文化民主化。有相机不等于有技能，能上传也不等于能被看到。文章最后提出了一个可以检验的预测。

Read with参考Benjamin, Hargittai and Walejko, Gillespie, Nancy FraserBenjamin、Hargittai 与 Walejko、Gillespie、Nancy Fraser
