I study how media shapes what people notice, feel and decide.我研究媒体怎样影响人们注意什么、感受什么、决定什么。

My courses in digital media theory, world cinemas, social psychology, and judgment and decision making keep coming back to one question: how do images, platforms and messages change what people pay attention to and choose? I’ve worked on it as a researcher and as a marketer.

数字媒体理论、世界电影、社会心理学、判断与决策，这几门课最后都落在同一个问题上：图像、平台和信息怎样改变人们关注什么、选择什么？我在研究里琢磨过它，在做宣传时也天天碰到它。

Outside class I cosplay, do character and event makeup, and play electric guitar and sing in DeADline, a band based in Kingston.

课外，我做 cosplay，给角色和活动化妆，还在金斯顿的乐队 DeADline 弹电吉他、唱主唱。
